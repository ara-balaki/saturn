Saturn
======

1. Drag Saturn into Applications.

2. Open it once from Applications. macOS says it can't check Saturn for malicious
   software, because Saturn isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID. Click Done.

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to the line about Saturn. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

From then on Saturn opens like any other app.

Saturn runs Claude Code in its terminals, so install that first:
https://claude.com/claude-code
